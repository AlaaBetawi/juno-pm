## What it demonstrates

Turns messy product inputs into structured, evidence-backed product requirements and insights.

## Debrief

- **What worked:** The AI successfully organized raw inputs, identified key insights, and generated clear product recommendations.
- **What broke / felt like a toy:** Some outputs were too generic and still required PM review and validation.
- **What I'd change next pass:** Improve source traceability, add stronger validation, and integrate directly with tools like Jira and Slack.
